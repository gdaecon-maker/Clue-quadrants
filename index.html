Quad one out

Guess the word. Each square hides one clue: a rhyme, an antonym, an anagram or a word that often goes before or after the answer in a phrase. (If the words are tea, fast, beat and me, for example, the answer would be eat.) You are not told which is which, and they change places every puzzle. You have three guesses. Good luck.

Your path

You start with 9 points. The first clue you open is free; each further clue costs 2 points and each wrong guess costs 1.
